About SOS Guide

📋 Our Mission

SOS Guide centralizes citizen assistance and emergency numbers for quick access in any situation. Our goal is to provide universal access to vital information: emergency services, social assistance, local associations, and citizen resources.

SOS Guide ensures this information remains available even without an internet connection, during network outages, natural disasters, or in areas with no coverage.

⚙️ Advanced Technical Operation

📶 Local Wi-Fi Network

The Raspberry Pi broadcasts its own "SOS-GUIDE" Wi-Fi network. No password required for quick connection.

💾 Local Storage

All data is stored on a microSD card. No internet connection is needed once the system is started.

🔋 Low Consumption

The Raspberry Pi consumes only 3-5 watts. It can run on an external battery for 24+ hours.

👥 Multi-user

Supports up to 50 simultaneous users on the local network. Ideal for public places and communities.

🏠 Kit Design

Storage: Store your kit in an easily accessible place, protected from moisture and direct light.

Check: Regularly check expiration dates (every 6 months).

Evacuation: Plan a backpack per person (30-40L) for quick evacuation.

Personalization: Adapt your kit to specific needs (babies, elderly, pets, special medications).

💡 Expert advice: Prepare two kits: a more complete "home kit" and a lightweight "evacuation kit" in a backpack. Regularly test your equipment and train all family members in its use.

5-Step Emergency Protocol

1 ASSESS the situation

Stay calm - Breathe deeply before acting

Analyze dangers: fire, smoke, collapse, gas, electricity

Protect yourself first before rescuing others

Identify emergency exits

Assess number of victims and their apparent condition

2 ALERT emergency services

Dial the appropriate number: 15 (SAMU), 18 (Firefighters), 17 (Police), 112 (EU)

Give this information clearly:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Exact address (city, street, number, floor, entry code)
  • Nature of the problem (accident, fire, illness...)
  • Number of victims and their apparent condition
  • Specific dangers (gas, chemicals, electricity...)
  • Actions already taken

Don't hang up first - Wait for the rescuer's instructions

3 PROTECT people

Move victims to safety away from danger

Mark the dangerous area to prevent other accidents

Turn off gas and electricity if necessary and safe

Keep bystanders away to facilitate emergency services

Establish a safety perimeter of at least 50 meters

4 RESCUE victims

Check consciousness: talk to the victim, shake gently

Check breathing: see if chest/stomach rises

Practice basic first aid:

  • Recovery position (if unconscious but breathing)
  • Compress heavy bleeding (with clean cloth)
  • Cardiopulmonary resuscitation if trained (30 compressions/2 breaths)
  • Use defibrillator if available (AED)

Warm victims with blankets or clothing

Never give drink to an unconscious person

5 WAIT for emergency services

Prepare access: clear corridors, open doors

Signal your presence: turn on lights, make signs

Transmit information to emergency services upon arrival

Follow their instructions without interfering

Stay available to provide additional information

Note arrival time of emergency services for follow-up

🎓 Recommended training: Take a PSC1 (First Aid and Civil Protection Level 1) course to master these skills. These courses are accessible to everyone from age 10 and last about 7 hours.

Emergency Alert and Communication

📱 Official Alert Systems

SAIP (Population Alert and Information System):

  • Free "SAIP" mobile app to download
  • Push notifications for local alerts (floods, incidents, attacks)
  • Real-time information with behavior advice
  • Geolocation for targeted alerts

FR-Alert: cell broadcast system

  • Alerts automatically received on all mobile phones
  • Works without app or internet
  • Messages in case of immediate danger (via cell broadcast)
  • National coverage activated since 2022

Weather France Alerts: Meteorological vigilance

  • 4-color vigilance map (green, yellow, orange, red)
  • Alert bulletins for dangerous phenomena
  • 24h and 48h forecasts

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Communication Plan

Establish a plan before the crisis:

  1. Designate an out-of-region contact: The whole family calls this person to centralize information
  2. Plan meeting points: One near your home, one outside the neighborhood, one outside the city
  3. Use SMS rather than calls: SMS works better when the network is saturated
  4. Save battery: Power saving mode, portable chargers
  5. Short and clear messages: "I am safe at [location]", "Need help at [address]"
  6. Family code: Password or phrase to authenticate messages

⚠️ National Alert Signals

National alert network sirens:

  • Alert: Modulated sound rising and falling (3 times 1 minute separated by 5 seconds)
  • End of alert: Continuous sound for 30 seconds
  • Monthly test: First Wednesday of the month at noon (single sound of 1 minute 41 seconds)

Voice messages: in transport, public places, shopping centers

Variable message signs: on roads and highways

Phone alarm: Specific strident sound for FR-Alert

💡 In case of communication blackout: Use written messages (paper), visual signals (sheets in windows), whistle blows, or SOS Guide messaging system if available locally.

🆘 Life-Saving Actions - Complete Guide

🩸 Severe Bleeding

1. Press directly on the wound with clean cloth

2. Lay the victim down to prevent fainting

3. Elevate the limb if bleeding is on a limb

4. Compress the artery upstream if bleeding persists (pressure points)

5. Call 15 immediately for heavy bleeding

6. Apply a tourniquet only as last resort (note application time)

🔥 Burn

1. Cool for 15-20 minutes under lukewarm water (15-25°C)

2. Remove clothing and jewelry before swelling appears

3. Do not pop blisters (risk of infection)

4. Do not apply greasy substances (butter, oil, cream)

5. Cover with clean cloth and dry

6. Call 15 if: extensive burn (> palm size), on face, hands, joints, or chemical/electrical burn

😵 Illness / Loss of consciousness

1. Check consciousness: "Are you okay? Can you hear me?" + gentle stimulation

2. Check breathing: see if chest rises, listen, feel breath

3. If unconscious but breathing: Put in Recovery Position

4. If unconscious and not breathing: Start CPR (30 compressions/2 breaths)

5. Loosen collar, tie, belt

6. Protect from heat/cold with a blanket

7. Call 15 describing symptoms precisely

🤕 Trauma / Fracture

1. Immobilize the limb in the position found

2. Apply cold to reduce swelling and pain

3. Elevate the limb if possible

4. Do not give anything to eat or drink (risk of surgery)

5. Watch for signs of shock: pallor, sweating, chills, agitation

6. Transport only if necessary, with prior immobilization

📋 Family Medical Preparation

  • List of usual medications with precise dosage and treatment duration
  • Copy of current prescriptions (to renew before expiration)
  • Professional contacts: GP, specialists, on-duty pharmacy
  • Medical information: blood type, known allergies (medications, food, others)
  • Document on chronic conditions: diabetes, hypertension, asthma, epilepsy, etc.
  • Up-to-date vaccination record for all family members
  • Health insurance: policy numbers, phones, coverage
  • ICE contacts (In Case of Emergency) programmed in the phone

Fundamental Rights & Duties

⚖️ Foundations of the Republic

🇫🇷 Constitution of 1958

Fundamental law organizing public powers, defining freedoms and citizens' rights. Establishes the Fifth Republic.

📜 Declaration of the Rights of Man (1789)

"Men are born and remain free and equal in rights." 17 founding articles of natural and inalienable rights.

🤝 Preamble of 1946

Establishes social and economic rights: right to work, health, education, gender equality.

🌈 Environmental Charter (2005)

Integrates environmental protection in the Constitution: right to live in a balanced and healthy environment.

🛡️ Your Fundamental Rights

🧑‍⚖️ Civil and political rights

Freedom of expression, conscience, religion. Right to vote, association, peaceful demonstration.

🏥 Social and economic rights

Right to work, health, education, housing, social protection, adequate standard of living.

👶 Specific rights

Children's rights, rights of persons with disabilities, elderly rights, gender equality.

⚖️ Legal rights

Presumption of innocence, right to fair trial, right to lawyer, protection against arbitrariness.

🌍 Environmental rights

Right to healthy environment, information on risks, participation in environmental decisions.

📱 Digital rights

Personal data protection (GDPR), freedom of internet access, right to be forgotten.

🤝 Your Citizen Duties

🏛️ Civic duties

Respect the law, pay taxes, participate in elections, serve as juror if summoned.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Social duties

Respect others' rights, protect vulnerable persons, contribute to national solidarity.

🎖️ Civil duties

Participate in Defense and Citizenship Day (JDC), respect symbols of the Republic.

🌳 Environmental duties

Contribute to environmental preservation, respect ecological regulations.

🆘 Duties in emergency situations

Assist persons in danger (duty to rescue), follow authorities' instructions.

📚 Educational duties

Ensure children's education, respect compulsory schooling (ages 3 to 16).
